# SokoniHub Responsible AI Deployment Architecture

### photos
SokoniHub strictly prohibits the automated scraping or continuous ingestion of raw listing photography that contains identifiable human faces, public marketplace crowds, or minors. The image data ingest pipeline treats visual objects exclusively as physical retail inventory metrics. Any uploaded metadata tag referencing a person, face, or child triggers an automated privacy flag that quarantines the asset for manual human verification before it can enter the cross-modal dataset. No actual images of minors are stored or committed to version histories.

### language
The platform treats localized phrasing, traditional handicraft definitions, and regional language variations as critical first-class data elements rather than parsing errors. Our cross-modal engine uses explicit sidecar keyword expansion layers to prevent international semantic models from misinterpreting authentic Kenyan trade asset indicators (such as unique handwoven basket categories). We ensure that automated listing reviews remain transparent, avoiding silent string overwrites or modifications to user descriptions behind the scenes.

### generated pixels
All synthetic media assets, placeholder still-life illustrations, and generative models are isolated from the golden training catalog datasets. Any artificial output file produced by testing stubs or design templates is restricted to the outputs directory and stamped with unalterable tracking metadata reading "not_a_photo_of_the_sku" to guarantee provenance clarity. The system does not use prompt strings designed to generate realistic human faces or replicate branded trademark imagery.

### review path
The automated cross-modal scoring engine operates strictly as an alerting mechanism for users rather than an autonomous judge. High-risk mismatch flags do not trigger automatic product deletions or immediate account bans. Flagged listings are seamlessly routed to a dedicated internal workspace where marketplace operations personnel perform manual side-by-side reviews. The system is designed to provide helpful recommendations, leaving all final enforcement actions to human platform review teams.

### Module Development Management Context
*   System Risk Vectors: The primary operational risk stems from cross-modal alignment failures, where mismatched inventory details could disrupt marketplace discovery patterns.
*   Platform Mitigations: We have deployed deterministic parsing layers, sidecar verification metrics, and clear data logging mechanisms to guarantee transparency across all automated system checks.
*   Statutory Privacy Alignment: The framework incorporates privacy tags and strict object segregation to ensure data handling practices align with regional statutory data protection principles.
*   What We Do Not Claim: We do not claim this framework is complete, absolute, or unhackable. It serves as a bounded operational analysis utility. We do not claim automated systems replace human validation layers for specialized trade products.
*   Cross-Module Transition Notes: The systems engineered within this framework are dedicated entirely to SokoniHub retail catalog metrics. The medical triage fixtures developed during the prior AfyaPlus system modules remain completely separate and have not been mixed into this commercial framework. The transition of marketing posters and informational leaflets will be handled independently in a subsequent engineering phase.
